●The Magic of a Simple Square

At its heart, a QR code is a bridge between the physical world and the digital one. It's a clever way to pack information—a website link, a Wi-Fi password, a secret message—into a simple black-and-white pattern that your phone's camera can read instantly. No more awkwardly typing a long URL from a poster or business card.

The real beauty of the QR code is its elegant simplicity and ubiquity. It's a universal standard that works everywhere, on everything from restaurant menus to event tickets. This tool gives you the power to create your own little digital doorways, for free and with zero fuss.
