▸Generate Subtitles in 3 Easy Steps

                    

                        
Transform any video into subtitle files with automatic speech recognition technology.

                                ❶
                                
    
                                    Upload Your Video
                                    
    Drag and drop your video file into the upload area or click to select. Supports all common video formats including MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, and more.
    
                                
    
                            
    • 
                            
  • 
                                ❷
                                
    
                                    Configure Settings
                                    
    Select the language of your video's audio and choose the processing model quality. Higher quality models are more accurate but take longer to process.

  • 
                                ❸
                                
    
                                    Generate and Download
                                    
    Click 'Generate Subtitles' and wait for the processing to complete. Once done, preview your subtitles and download the SRT file.
