▸How to Use

Follow these simple steps to find duplicate words in your text. All processing is done securely in your browser.

  • ❶
    Paste Your Text: Copy and paste your text into the input box on the left.
  • ❷
    Set Options: Choose whether the search should be case-sensitive.
  • ❸
    Find Duplicates: Click the "Find Duplicates" button to see a list of repeated words and their counts.

✦Key Features

Our Duplicate words Finder is designed for accuracy, privacy, and ease of use. Here's what makes it stand out:

  • ◆
    Completely Private: All processing happens in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to a server, ensuring 100% privacy and security.
  • ⚡
    Instant Results: The tool provides immediate feedback, listing all duplicate words and their frequencies as soon as you click the button.
  • ◆
    Case-Sensitive Search: You have the option to perform a case-sensitive search to differentiate between words like "The" and "the".
  • ●
    Clear and Simple Interface: The tool is designed to be intuitive and easy to use, with a clean layout that presents the information clearly.
