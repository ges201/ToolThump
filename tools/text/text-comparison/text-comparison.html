▸How to Compare Texts

Finding the differences between two texts is simple with this tool.

  • ❶
    Paste Your Texts

    Copy the original text and paste it into the 'Text 1' box. Then, paste the new or modified text into the 'Text 2' box.

  • ❷
    Click Compare

    Press the 'Compare Texts' button. The tool will analyze both texts and highlight the differences in the results area below.

  • ❸
    Review the Differences

    In the results, you'll see words that were added highlighted in green, and words that were removed will be in red with a strikethrough. This makes it easy to spot the changes.

✦Key Features

This tool is designed for accuracy and ease of use.

  • ◆
    Client-Side Comparison

    All comparisons happen in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server, ensuring your data remains private.

  • ◆
    Word-by-Word Highlighting

    The tool highlights differences at the word level, making it easy to see exactly what has been added or removed.

  • ●
    Completely Free

    This is a free tool. No sign-up or payment is required.
