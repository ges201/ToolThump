▸How to Use

Follow these simple steps to effectively crop and resize your images. All processing is done securely in your browser.

  • ❶
    Upload Your Image

    Click the "Choose Image" button or drag and drop your image onto the preview area. Your image will appear in the main canvas.

  • ❷
    Adjust Crop & Resize Options

    Choose between "Free Crop" to manually select any area, or "Fixed Ratio" for predefined aspect ratios (e.g., 1:1, 16:9). You can also input target dimensions.

  • ❸
    Process & Download

    Once satisfied with your selection, click "Process & Download Image". The tool will crop and resize your image, then automatically download it to your device.

✦Key Features

This Image Resizer & Cropper is designed for efficiency, privacy, and ease of use. Here's what makes it stand out:

  • ◆
    Client-Side Processing

    All image manipulation happens directly in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any servers, ensuring complete privacy and fast processing.

  • ✕
    Flexible Cropping

    Use the intuitive cropping interface to select any portion of your image. Adjust the crop box freely or lock it to popular aspect ratios.

  • ▨
    Precise Resizing

    Easily resize images to exact pixel dimensions. Maintain aspect ratio or set custom width and height for specific needs.

  • ■
    Transparency Support

    Preserves transparency from PNG files. When you crop or resize a transparent PNG, the output will also be a transparent PNG, not a flattened image with a white background.

  • ⚡
    Instant Download

    Once processed, your new image is immediately available for download. No waiting, no extra steps.

  • ■
    Multiple Formats

    Supports common image formats like JPEG, PNG, and WebP, allowing you to work with a variety of image files.
