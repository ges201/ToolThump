▸How to Convert Your Images

Converting images is a straightforward process. You can convert a single file, multiple files, or an entire folder of images right in your browser.

  • ❶
    Select Your Image(s)

    Drag and drop an image, multiple images, or a folder directly into the upload area. Alternatively, use the 'Select Files' or 'Select Folder' buttons to browse your device.

  • ❷
    Choose the Output Format

    Use the dropdown menu to select your desired format: PNG, JPEG, WEBP, or GIF.

  • ❸
    Convert and Download

    Click the 'Convert & Download' button. A single converted image will download directly. Multiple images or a folder will be zipped up for a single, convenient download.

✦Powerful Features, Total Privacy

This converter is built to be fast, private, and flexible, handling all your conversion needs without ever uploading a single file.

  • ◆
    100% Client-Side & Private

    All image processing happens in your browser. Your files are never sent to a server, ensuring your data remains completely private and secure.

  • ●
    Bulk Folder Conversion

    Save time by converting an entire folder of images at once. The tool recursively finds all supported images (JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF) in all subdirectories.

  • ●
    Retain Folder Structure

    When converting a folder, you can choose to keep the original subfolder hierarchy in the output ZIP file, keeping your project perfectly organized.

  • ⚡
    Fast and Efficient

    By using your computer's processing power, conversions are completed in seconds, not minutes. No queues, no waiting.

  • ○
    Automatic Transparency Handling

    When converting from a format with transparency (like PNG) to one without (like JPEG), the background is automatically filled with white for a clean result.

◆Your In-Browser Conversion Engine

This tool transforms your web browser into a powerful image processing utility. Unlike traditional online converters that require you to upload your files to a remote server, this tool leverages the capabilities of modern browsers to perform all the work locally on your machine.

This client-side approach means your sensitive images stay with you, eliminating privacy risks and the delays associated with uploading and downloading. Whether you're converting a single photo or a large batch of images for a project, the process is fast, secure, and entirely under your control.
